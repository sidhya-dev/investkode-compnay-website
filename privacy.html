Privacy Policy

Effective date: 15 September 2026  ·  Last updated: 21 September 2026

Your privacy matters. This policy explains what data InvestKode AI collects, how we use it, and your rights. We do not sell your personal data to third parties.

1. Who We Are

InvestKode AI is an investment research platform that helps investors track company updates, filings, earnings events, and market news. InvestKode is operated by FinAI Global, a partnership firm with its registered address at A171, Brookhaven, JVLR, Mumbai 400060, Maharashtra, India. We can be reached at support@investkode.ai.

2. Information We Collect

We collect the following categories of information when you use InvestKode AI:

  • Account information: name and email address provided through your Google account when you sign in
  • Google Calendar data: calendar events accessed or created via the Google Calendar API (see Section 4)
  • Usage data: pages visited, features used, and interactions with our platform
  • Device information: browser type, operating system, and IP address for security and analytics
  • Communications: messages you send us via email or support channels
  • Payment information: payments are processed by PayU. We receive the transaction ID, amount and payment status, not your card or bank credentials

3. How We Use Your Information

We use the information we collect to:

  • Provide, operate, and improve the InvestKode AI platform
  • Add company events (earnings, AGMs, analyst days) to your Google Calendar
  • Send you relevant company updates and alerts you subscribe to
  • Respond to your support requests and communications
  • Detect and prevent fraud, abuse, or security incidents
  • Comply with legal obligations

We do not use your data for advertising or sell it to third parties.

4. Google API Services — Limited Use & AI/ML Disclosure

InvestKode AI integrates with Google APIs, including the Google Calendar API, to provide automated calendar event scheduling and reminder features. Our use and transfer to any other app of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy and the Google Workspace API User Data and Developer Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Google API Scopes Requested

Scope Purpose
https://www.googleapis.com/auth/calendar.events Create, update, and remove investment concall reminders on your primary calendar
https://www.googleapis.com/auth/userinfo.email Verify account connection and display your connected Google email in settings
https://www.googleapis.com/auth/userinfo.profile Display your profile identity during sign-in

We access your Google Calendar solely to write, update, or remove investment-related events that you track. We do not read, harvest, or log your existing personal calendar events, meeting contents, or attendee lists.

Limited Use & Artificial Intelligence (AI/ML) Policy

• No AI/ML Model Training: Google Workspace user data (including any Google Calendar data, event IDs, and metadata) is never used to develop, train, tune, or improve generalized or non-personalized artificial intelligence or machine learning models. Google Calendar data is strictly segregated and air-gapped from our AI research tools, LLM Gateway, and Large Language Models (LLMs).

• No Third-Party AI Transfers: Google Workspace user data is never transferred, sold, or disclosed to third-party AI model providers (including OpenAI, Azure OpenAI, Anthropic, or OpenRouter) or external data brokers.

• No Advertising: Google Workspace user data is never used or transferred for serving personalized, retargeted, or interest-based advertisements.

• Human Access Restrictions: Human employees or contractors are strictly prohibited from reading your Google Calendar data, unless: (1) you have provided explicit permission for resolving a specific technical support request; (2) it is required for security or anti-abuse investigations; (3) required to comply with applicable laws; or (4) the data is aggregated and anonymized for internal system health operations.

5. Data Sharing

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information. We may share data only in the following limited circumstances:

  • Service providers: trusted vendors who help us operate the platform (e.g. hosting, analytics), bound by confidentiality obligations
  • Legal requirements: when required by applicable law, court order, or governmental authority in India
  • Business transfers: in connection with a merger, acquisition, or sale of assets, with notice to you
  • With your consent: for any other purpose with your explicit permission

6. Data Retention

We retain your personal data for as long as your account is active or as needed to provide our services. You may request deletion of your account and associated data at any time by emailing IT@investkode.ai. We will delete or anonymize your data within 30 days of a verified request, unless retention is required by law.

Data received from Google APIs is retained strictly as necessary to support the calendar sync feature. We store encrypted OAuth authorization tokens to maintain active synchronization and reference IDs for events created by InvestKode AI so they can be updated or removed if rescheduled. We do not store or harvest the contents of your existing personal calendar events. Upon account deletion or calendar disconnection, stored OAuth tokens and event mappings are permanently deleted from our databases.

7. Data Security

We implement industry-standard security measures including encrypted data transmission (TLS), access controls, and regular security reviews. However, no method of transmission over the internet is completely secure. We encourage you to use a strong password and notify us immediately of any suspected unauthorized access.

8. Your Rights

You have the right to:

  • Access the personal data we hold about you
  • Request correction of inaccurate data
  • Request deletion of your data
  • Revoke Google permissions at any time via your Google Account settings
  • Withdraw consent where processing is based on consent

To exercise any of these rights, contact us at IT@investkode.ai.

9. Cookies and Tracking

We use essential cookies to maintain your session and preferences. We may use analytics tools to understand how the platform is used in aggregate. You can control cookie settings through your browser, though disabling essential cookies may affect platform functionality.

10. Children's Privacy

InvestKode AI is not directed at children under 18. We do not knowingly collect personal information from minors. If you believe a minor has provided us with personal data, please contact us and we will delete it promptly.

11. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of significant changes by updating the "Last updated" date above and, where appropriate, by email. Continued use of InvestKode AI after changes constitutes acceptance of the revised policy.

12. Governing Law

This Privacy Policy is governed by the laws of India, including the Information Technology Act, 2000 and its rules. Any disputes shall be subject to the jurisdiction of courts in India.

Questions or privacy requests? Write to us at support@investkode.ai
