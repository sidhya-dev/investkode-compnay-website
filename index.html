The Opening Truth

Investment intelligence today is not evenly distributed.

Large institutions, corporates, and professional investors operate with access to decades of investment banking expertise, deep research teams, and disciplined frameworks.

Retail investors, on the other hand, participate in the same markets — but without access to the same quality of thinking or knowledge.

This is not because retail investors lack interest or intent. It's because the intelligence itself has never been designed for them.

This inequality quietly compounds — year after year — and leads to a very low equity asset base for Indian investors as a percentage of their financial assets.

"People invest when they understand. People stay away when they don't."

This is not a behavioural problem.
This is a structural intelligence problem.
