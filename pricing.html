How Billing Works

No surprises. Cancel anytime.

1

Start your 15-day free trial

Every new Pro subscription starts with a 15-day free trial. Add a payment method to start — you are not charged during the trial.

2

We remind you before your trial ends

We email you a reminder at least 24 hours before the trial ends, so you are never caught off guard.

3

First charge on Day 16

If you do not cancel before the trial ends, the plan fee is charged on Day 16 and your subscription begins. Monthly billing from that date.

4

Auto-renews monthly

Subscriptions renew automatically at the end of each billing period unless you cancel. Cancel any time from Settings > Billing. Access continues to the end of the paid period.

5

Secure payments via PayU

Payments are processed securely by PayU. We accept UPI, credit and debit cards, and net banking. We do not store your card details.
